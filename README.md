# Superstores Finance and Sales Dashboard

An end-to-end data analysis and visualization project built to analyze financial performance, regional sales, and order trends for a retail superstore.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides key insights into the company's financial and sales performance across multiple dimensions, including geography, product categories, shipping modes, and monthly trends.

### **Key Metrics & KPIs**
* **Total Sales:** $2.30M
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 38K units
* **Total Profit:** $286.40K
* **Total Orders:** 5K
* **Performance Ratio:** 12.47%
* **Sum of Discount:** 1.56K

---

## 📈 Key Visualizations & Insights

1. **Sum of Sales by Region and Category:** Breaks down revenue across West, East, Central, and South regions, segmented by **Furniture**, **Office Supplies**, and **Technology**.
2. **Sum of Sales by Ship Mode:** Illustrates distribution across Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day shipping.
3. **Sum of Sales by Month:** Highlights seasonality and revenue trends throughout the year (noting significant peaks toward Q4).
4. **Sum of Sales by Sub-Category:** Ranks product lines from Phones and Chairs down to Envelopes.
5. **Profit Breakdown:** Provides granular tabular views of profit contributions across different **Cities** and **States**.
